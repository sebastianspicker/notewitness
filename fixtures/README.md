# Fixtures

`synthetic_lesson/project.json` and `synthetic_lesson/script.txt` are synthetic contract fixtures.
They contain no lesson recording, real participant, participant identifier, or model output.

The fixture project declares `CC0-1.0` in its synthetic rights record to exercise the schema. That
record is test data, not by itself a license grant for the fixture files. The repository has not
added a separate CC0 dedication. Until the maintainer confirms the terms, do not redistribute the
fixture as a separately licensed dataset or describe it as a published benchmark.

These fixtures validate deterministic evidence-graph and projection behavior. They do not prove
media playback, model installation, model accuracy, fairness, or corpus suitability.
