# Code of conduct

NoteWitness is a research-oriented community. Participation must be respectful,
evidence-led, and safe for people whose work may involve sensitive teaching and
research contexts.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors and research participants with dignity.
- Welcome correction, state uncertainty, and critique ideas without attacking
  people.
- Protect private or restricted material, and use synthetic reproductions
  whenever possible.
- Do not harass, discriminate, intimidate, sexualize, dox, or publish another
  person's private information.

## Enforcement

Do not publish sensitive conduct reports in an issue. Set up a private contact
channel with a maintainer before sharing details. If the report concerns that
maintainer, ask another project owner or the repository host for a private
conflict-of-interest route.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when
needed to protect participants. Handle reports privately, and disclose or
reassign conflicts of interest.
